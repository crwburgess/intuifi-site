How does it actually work, and what does it take from me?
You ask one or two people on your team to export the data from whatever tools you use, such as your CRM, support platform or project tracker. That's your part, the ask itself. Send the export to me and I'll run the analysis, then get a structured report back to you, usually within a day or two of receiving your data. You start with a high level summary and can drill down into the evidence behind any finding whenever you need to. Once you sign up, I'll send you a short note you can forward to whoever's doing the export.
Do I have to email you the file?
No. The note you forward includes a secure upload link, so whoever does the export can use that instead of email.
What results can I expect?
The report opens with an executive summary of the key themes and tensions across your data. Each theme comes with supporting evidence, an implication for your business, and signals to track. For example, a theme might read "Renewal risk rising in 3 accounts", with the evidence behind it (matching signals from CRM, support tickets and the engineering backlog) linked directly beneath, and an implication such as "the related fix is already logged in the backlog but hasn't been prioritised." Everything links back to the source so you can verify what matters. To see exactly what you get, take a look at this sample report.
How much data do I need?
A few months of support tickets, or a CRM export covering your active accounts, is usually enough to see real patterns. You don't need your whole history from every system. More sources and more time just make the picture fuller.
Who sees the report first?
I do. I check every report before it's sent, partly for quality, partly because I'm still learning what's working at this stage. After that, it comes to you and only you. You decide who else on your team sees it.
What does this cost?
Nothing right now. In return, I'll ask you for a short call after your first report to hear what worked and what didn't. When Intuifi moves to paid pricing, you'll know well in advance before anything changes for you.

How is my data handled?
I review the data behind every report I generate, to run the analysis and troubleshoot anything that doesn't process correctly. Your report and the underlying data are stored on MindStudio's servers, hosted on AWS in the United States, encrypted at rest and in transit, and are accessible to you via Intuifi using the email address you signed up with. MindStudio uses Anthropic's models under a commercial licence to run the analysis, and neither MindStudio nor Anthropic use your data to train their models. MindStudio is SOC 2 Type I and Type II certified. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

How accurate is the output?
That depends on the quality of the data you upload. Intuifi analyses what is there and surfaces the patterns it finds across it. It will not always get every theme right, and some findings may reflect noise rather than signal. That is why every finding links back to the source data, so you can check what looks right, discard what does not, and act with confidence on what remains. The analysis is a starting point, not a verdict.
How is this different from a typical dashboard?
This is not another dashboard. Dashboards usually present quantitative data and leave the interpretation to you. Intuifi works differently. It takes qualitative evidence like CRM records, support tickets, product backlogs and notes, then turns it into a structured report with clear themes, strategic implications, and the evidence behind each one. You are not exploring charts. You are seeing what is happening across the evidence your teams already hold, and why it matters.
Why would I not just use ChatGPT or Claude?
You shouldn't have to spend your time prompt engineering just to understand what is happening across your business. You still have to prepare your data, structure your questions, interpret the output, and turn it into something another person can review and trust. Intuifi removes that friction. Upload your files as they are and get back a structured report with themes, priorities, and the evidence behind every finding.
Why not just get my team to build this?

Vibe coding tools make it easier than ever to build something that generates an initial output. The harder part is making it consistent, objective, and trustworthy enough to guide strategic business decisions. Doing it in-house brings back the exact problems you are trying to fix:

  • Unbiased truth: You need an objective picture of what is happening across your business. If an internal team writes the rules for how data gets analysed, they inevitably control what gets highlighted and what gets buried, whether intentionally or not. This is particularly true when feedback points to issues with their own team's work.
  • Ongoing maintenance: In-house scripts rarely last more than a few months. As file formats change and new software is introduced, internal tools quietly break. Keeping a side project working will never take priority over your main product roadmap, so the tool is eventually abandoned.
  • Team focus: Your engineers should spend their time building your core product and growing the business, not fixing broken file imports, tweaking prompts, or checking inaccurate outputs.
Won't this become obsolete as AI improves?
The opposite. As AI becomes more capable, so does Intuifi. The methodology that structures your data, preserves the evidence, and produces something your team can trust is built on top of AI, not instead of it. A more powerful engine makes the output better, not unnecessary. What stays constant is the structure that makes the insight trustworthy and actionable.
