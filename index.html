What results can I expect?

A snapshot of what keeps coming up in your team's own material. It starts with what was read and a short headline, then a table of themes ranked by how often each one appears, with its share and when it was first and last seen.

Under each theme are two or three real examples, with names replaced by roles, each linked to the original item so you can check it. From there you can ask for a leadership write-up, the full evidence file, or everything behind any one theme.

How much data do I need?

A few months of support tickets, or a CRM export covering your active accounts, is usually enough to see real patterns. You don't need your whole history from every system. More sources and more time just make the picture fuller.

What does this cost?

The prompt is free. It runs inside the AI assistant you already use, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing extra to pay.

The next version, with memory, will be a paid product. Pricing isn't set yet. If you register interest, you'll hear about it before anything changes.

How is my data handled?

The prompt runs entirely inside your own AI assistant account. It reads only the tools you have connected and any files you attach, and nothing is sent to Intuifi or to me. Your data is handled under the same terms as everything else in that account.

Before showing any excerpt, the prompt removes email addresses and phone numbers and replaces names of people and organisations with their role or type, so the snapshot is safer to share.

When the memory version arrives, your raw data will still stay in your account. Intuifi will keep only theme names and counts.

How accurate is the output?

That depends on the quality of the data you upload. Intuifi analyses what is there and surfaces the patterns it finds across it. It will not always get every theme right, and some findings may reflect noise rather than signal. That is why every finding links back to the source data, so you can check what looks right, discard what does not, and act with confidence on what remains. The analysis is a starting point, not a verdict.

Why not just get my team to build this?

You could, and the prompt is a fair place to start, since you can read every line of it. Getting a first answer is easy. Getting a reliable one every time is the hard part: counts that are calculated rather than estimated, every claim linked to its source, names kept out, and all of it holding up as your tools and data change.

That takes ongoing tuning, and it will rarely be the priority for a team building your core product. Intuifi does that work so your team doesn't have to.

Won't this become obsolete as AI improves?

Better AI makes Intuifi better, not unnecessary. It runs inside the assistant you already use, so every time that assistant improves, so does your snapshot.

What Intuifi adds is the method around it: what to read, how to count, how to keep evidence linked and names out, and next, a memory of how things change over time. A smarter assistant doesn't keep that record for you on its own.
