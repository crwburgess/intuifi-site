Recommended actions

Listed in priority order, most urgent first.

1. Call Brightline Technologies first, and treat it differently to the other two

Brightline Technologies isn't just one of three quiet accounts, it's also one of five accounts your sales team uses as a reference, and it's already mentioned reviewing other options once. Call this week and ask directly about renewal. Solace Software and Ashford Digital's silence doesn't carry the same signal and can follow after.

Owner: Customer success lead

2. Fix the Xero export before the scheduling conflict

Both bugs are scoped, but Xero hits every account on that integration with certainty, including Ridgemont Technologies, who is also waiting on the team-level reporting feature. The fix is already in the backlog (ENG-388), so it needs moving up, not scoping. Clearing their invoicing issue first is also the fastest way to earn the right to ask them to wait on that feature.

Owner: Product lead

3. Hold team-level reporting back for two accounts, not all five

Vantage Systems, Meridian Cloud and Larkfield Data aren't blocked by anything else in this report, so ship it for them now. Ridgemont Technologies and Brightline Technologies each have a live, unresolved issue of their own, so give them a firm date once that's fixed rather than a yes today.

Owner: Product lead

Detailed themes and evidence

Resource scheduling conflicts in multi-team accounts

Implementation specialists are being double-booked when two active onboarding engagements at the same account overlap. It isn't happening everywhere: only accounts running more than one engagement at a time are affected.

Root cause20 pieces of evidence across 6 of 10 accounts33% of evidenceJun to Sept 2025

Decision this informs: Whether the fix belongs in how the calendar handles overlapping engagements specifically, rather than a general scheduling rebuild.

Show the evidence
Evidence trail

Why this theme emerged

01

Northlane Analytics: two specialists double-booked into the same session, right after taking on a second engagement. Still open (RS-2210-JUN, 11 Jun 2025). Support ticket

02

Fernbridge Software: same double-booking, starting only once they added a second engagement. No issues in the four months before that (RS-2299-JUL, 16 Jul 2025). Support ticket

03

Halcyon Cloud: same conflict within weeks of starting a second engagement. Follow-up required (RS-2355-AUG, 2 Aug 2025). Support ticket

04

Vantage Systems: heaviest single-engagement user on the platform, zero scheduling conflicts all period (RS-2401, 1 Sept 2025). Support ticket

05

20 pieces of evidence in total, all from accounts running two or more engagements at once. No single-engagement account reported it.

InterpretationWhat this suggests

This reads like a general reliability problem if you only look at volume, but it's actually a specific gap: the calendar doesn't handle two active engagements at the same account well. That's a smaller, more precise fix than “scheduling is broken”.

Signals to monitor
  • Scheduling conflicts, split by accounts running one engagement versus two or more
  • Time between an account starting a second concurrent engagement and their first scheduling conflict
  • Whether any single-engagement account reports this after the fix ships

Why this theme emerged

01

Solace Software: six tickets a month through July, then none since early August. Renews in eight weeks. Support ticket

02

Ashford Digital: dropped from four tickets a month to zero over the same window, despite being one of the more vocal accounts on invoicing. Support ticket

03

Brightline Technologies: mentioned “reviewing other options” on a check-in call. No follow-up was logged (RC-4102-AUG, 14 Aug 2025). Sales call notes

InterpretationWhat this suggests

Ticket volume dropping to zero usually gets read as good news. Here it lines up with renewal dates and, in one case, a comment about reviewing alternatives. That combination is worth a direct conversation, not a tally mark in the resolved column.

Signals to monitor
  • Ticket and call volume per account in the eight weeks before their renewal date
  • Any account whose volume drops to zero without a corresponding fix being shipped
  • Whether a check-in call happens with each of these three accounts before their renewal date
